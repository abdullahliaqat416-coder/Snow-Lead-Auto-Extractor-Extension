## 💡 Why Download Snowlead? (The Benefit)

Are you tired of manually opening hundreds of LinkedIn profiles, copying URLs, and pasting them into Excel sheets? **Manual data entry is dead.**

**Snowlead** is built to save you hours of boring, repetitive work. It is an intelligent, automated bot specifically designed for LinkedIn Recruiter Lite. You just need to click "Start," and the bot takes over. It scrolls the page, opens hidden profiles to extract real public URLs, saves the data safely in its memory, and automatically moves to the next page. 

**The ultimate benefit?** You get a perfectly formatted CSV file packed with high-quality leads while you sit back and relax. It does the work of an entire data-entry team for free!

---

## ⚙️ How It Works? (The Magic)

Snowlead operates exactly like a human user but at a much faster and more reliable pace. 
1. **Auto-Scrolling:** It scrolls down the Recruiter Lite search page to load all 25 candidates.
2. **Hidden URL Extraction:** Recruiter Lite hides public URLs. Snowlead automatically clicks on each profile, waits for the overlay to load, grabs the real public URL and Country, and closes the popup.
3. **Smart Pagination:** Once a page is completed, it finds the "Next Page" button, clicks it, and repeats the process infinitely until your search ends.

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Installation

Since this is a custom, open-source developer tool, it is not available on the Chrome Web Store. You can install it for free in **Developer Mode** by following these easy steps:

1. **Download the Code:** Click the green **`<> Code`** button at the top of this repository and select **`Download ZIP`**.
2. **Extract the Folder:** Unzip the downloaded file on your computer (Desktop or Documents).
3. **Open Extension Settings:** Open Google Chrome and type `chrome://extensions/` in the URL bar and press Enter.
4. **Enable Developer Mode:** In the top right corner of the screen, turn ON the **`Developer mode`** switch.
5. **Load the Extension:** Click the **`Load unpacked`** button on the top left. Select the extracted `Snowlead` folder from your computer.
6. **Pin it:** Click the puzzle icon in Chrome and pin 📌 Snowlead to your browser bar for quick access!

## 💻 How to Use the Extension

Using Snowlead is incredibly simple:
1. Log in to your **LinkedIn Recruiter Lite** account.
2. Search for your target audience (e.g., "Software Engineers in New York").
3. Click on the **Snowlead Extension Icon** to open the side panel.
4. Click the **`▶ Start auto-pilot`** button.
5. Do not close the tab! Watch the bot magically extract data.
6. Once you have enough leads or the bot finishes, click **`⏸ Stop bot`**, then click **`⬇ Download csv`**.
